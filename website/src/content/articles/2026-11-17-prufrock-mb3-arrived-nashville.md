On 17 November 2026 The Boring Company posted that Prufrock-MB3 has arrived in Nashville as the third tunnel boring machine for Music City Loop.

> Nashville, we’re hiring!  
> Prufrock-MB3 has arrived (the third TBM building Music City Loop), and Prufrock-MB4 arrives in the next 8 weeks.  
> Come dig with us - Engineering, Ops, Weld, Maintenance, Electrical, Facilities, Safety, and Security.  
> Hiring Event: Tuesday, November 24  
> Apply here:

## What was announced

- Prufrock-MB3 has arrived and is the third TBM building Music City Loop.
- Prufrock-MB4 is expected to arrive in the next 8 weeks.
- The company is hiring for Engineering, Ops, Weld, Maintenance, Electrical, Facilities, Safety, and Security roles.
- A hiring event is scheduled for Tuesday, 24 November.

## Context

Music City Loop is The Boring Company’s project in Nashville. Earlier official updates stated that Prufrock-MB1 and Prufrock-MB2 were actively tunneling.

## Limits of this report

This report contains only the details stated in the source post. It does not include launch dates for MB3, exact arrival timing for MB4 beyond the stated window, hiring numbers, or any operational metrics.
