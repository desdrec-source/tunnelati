On August 7, 2026, The Boring Company posted an announcement introducing Liner Truck 3, its latest fully electric tunnel vehicle.

> Introducing Liner Truck 3 — our latest fully electric tunnel vehicle.

## What was announced
- Tesla Model 3 battery and drive units
- Capacity to transport 22,000+ lb of concrete segments to the boring machine
- 28 miles of range
- 12 mph max operating speed
- Remotely piloted from Global OCC in Texas

## Context
The Boring Company develops tunnel boring machines and support vehicles for underground transport projects such as the Vegas Loop.
