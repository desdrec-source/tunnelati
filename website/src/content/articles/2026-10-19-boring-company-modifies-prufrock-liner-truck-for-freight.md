On 19 October 2026, The Boring Company posted that it is using its tunnels to transport freight, with the latest Prufrock Liner Truck modified to carry a standard 20-foot shipping container.

> Using TBC tunnels to transport freight.
>
> The latest Prufrock Liner Truck (using Tesla Model 3 batteries and drive units) has been modified to carry a standard 20’ shipping container.
>
> Fits like a glove!

## What was announced
- The company stated it is using TBC tunnels to transport freight.
- The latest Prufrock Liner Truck, which uses Tesla Model 3 batteries and drive units, has been modified to carry a standard 20-foot shipping container.
- In a follow-up post, the company stated: Rev 1 (current) payload is 45,000 lb. Rev 2 (January 2027) payload is 75,000 lb.

## Context
The Boring Company previously introduced Liner Truck 3 as an electric vehicle using Tesla Model 3 battery and drive units for transporting concrete segments to tunnel boring machines.

This report covers only the details stated in the source posts. It does not include operational timelines, specific tunnel locations, regulatory approvals, or performance metrics beyond the stated payloads.
