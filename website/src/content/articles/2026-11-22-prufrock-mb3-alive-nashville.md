On 22 November 2026 The Boring Company posted that Prufrock-MB3 is alive in Nashville for Music City Loop.

> Prufrock-MB3 is alive in Nashville!
>
> One week of commissioning/close-out, and will be tunneling by end of month. Two of the four grippers are visible on the right, each which push laterally with ~1 million pounds of force.
>
> Prufrock-MB4 then arrives in December and will be tunneling in January.

## What was announced

* Prufrock-MB3 is alive in Nashville.
* One week of commissioning and close-out is underway, after which it will be tunneling by the end of the month.
* Two of the four grippers are visible in the accompanying video; each pushes laterally with approximately 1 million pounds of force.
* Prufrock-MB4 is expected to arrive in December and begin tunneling in January.

## Context

Music City Loop is The Boring Company’s project in Nashville. Earlier official updates stated that Prufrock-MB1 and Prufrock-MB2 were actively tunneling, and that Prufrock-MB3 had arrived as the third machine.

## Limits of this report

This report contains only the details stated in the source post. It does not include exact tunneling start dates beyond the end-of-month window, launch-site specifics, progress metrics, or operational details for the other machines.
