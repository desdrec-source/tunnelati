On 22 November 2026 The Boring Company posted that Prufrock-MB3 is alive in Nashville.

> Prufrock-MB3 is alive in Nashville!
>
> One week of commissioning/close-out, and will be tunneling by end of month. Two of the four grippers are visible on the right, each which push laterally with ~1 million pounds of force.
>
> Prufrock-MB4 then arrives in December and will be tunneling in January.

## What was announced

- Prufrock-MB3 is alive in Nashville.
- One week of commissioning and close-out is underway, with tunneling expected by the end of the month.
- Two of the four grippers are visible; each pushes laterally with approximately 1 million pounds of force.
- Prufrock-MB4 is expected to arrive in December and begin tunneling in January.

## Context

Music City Loop is The Boring Company’s project in Nashville. Earlier official posts stated that Prufrock-MB1 and Prufrock-MB2 were actively tunneling and that Prufrock-MB3 had arrived as the third machine.

## Limits of this report

This report contains only the details stated in the source post. It does not include exact launch-site location, current depth or progress of other machines, precise commissioning steps, or any performance metrics beyond the gripper force given.
