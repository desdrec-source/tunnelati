The Boring Company published details on the construction of a cross-passage connecting two tunnels beneath Paradise Road and restated current Vegas Loop infrastructure counts.

> Vegas Loop isn’t just tunnels! In Vegas, besides 14 tunnels, TBC has built 14 stations (28 by end of year), 12 emergency egresses, 6 cross-passages, and 9 "knockout shafts".
>
> See below for the construction of a cross-passage connecting "Tunnel-1" and "Tunnel-2" (beneath Paradise Road) to provide access and egress; construction time is ~10 days.
>
> Steps:
> 1. If necessary, treat the ground (typically with grout) between the two tunnels such that it is stable during excavation.
> 2. Brace the Tunnel-1 wall with a steel frame and open up the concrete Tunnel-1 wall within said frame.
> 3. Excavate the dirt between the tunnels, installing steel ribs and wood lagging to maintain a safe roof until Tunnel-2 is exposed. This is a slow process of 10 feet per day.
> 4. Install waterproofing and rebar, the latter which anchors into both Tunnels 1 and 2 (see pic)
> 5. Install solid steel liner plate (see pic)
> 6. Pour high-strength concrete around the liner plate, reinforced with the rebar from Step 4
> 7. Open up Tunnel-2, finalizing the connection
>
> Finally, the cross-passage is built out with a walking surface, ventilation, fire suppression valves, fire-rated doors, safety equipment/sensors, lighting, and cameras.

— @boringcompany

## What was announced

- Current totals stated: 14 tunnels, 14 stations (with 28 stations by end of year), 12 emergency egresses, 6 cross-passages, and 9 knockout shafts.
- Detailed construction sequence for a cross-passage connecting Tunnel-1 and Tunnel-2 beneath Paradise Road.
- Stated construction time for the cross-passage is approximately 10 days.
- The finished cross-passage includes a walking surface, ventilation, fire suppression valves, fire-rated doors, safety equipment/sensors, lighting, and cameras.

## Context

The Vegas Loop is The Boring Company’s operational and expanding point-to-point underground transportation system in Las Vegas. The company has previously stated plans to reach 28 operational stations by the end of 2026 and has received entitlements for a larger network.
