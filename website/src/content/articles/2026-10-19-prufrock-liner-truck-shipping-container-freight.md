The Boring Company stated on 19 October 2026 that it is using TBC tunnels to transport freight and that the latest Prufrock Liner Truck has been modified to carry a standard 20’ shipping container.

> Using TBC tunnels to transport freight.
>
> The latest Prufrock Liner Truck (using Tesla Model 3 batteries and drive units) has been modified to carry a standard 20’ shipping container.
>
> Fits like a glove!

## What was announced

- The company is using TBC tunnels to transport freight.
- The latest Prufrock Liner Truck, which uses Tesla Model 3 batteries and drive units, has been modified to carry a standard 20’ shipping container.
- In a reply to the post, the company stated: “Rev 1 (current) payload is 45,000 lb. Rev 2 (January 2027) payload is 75,000 lb.”

## Context

The Prufrock Liner Truck is an electric tunnel vehicle previously introduced by The Boring Company for transporting concrete segments to tunnel boring machines. Earlier public posts described Liner Truck 3 as using Tesla Model 3 battery and drive units, with capacity to transport 22,000+ lb of concrete segments.

## Limits of this report

This report covers only the statements in the source post and the immediate official reply from @boringcompany. It does not include deployment locations, operational status, schedules beyond the stated January 2027 payload figure, comparisons with other vehicles, or any unstated technical specifications.
