The Boring Company announced that the Vegas Loop station count will double by the end of the year and that it is urgently hiring Drivers and Ops Managers for the project.

>

> Senior Loop Ops Manager

## What was announced

- The station count for Vegas Loop will double by the end of the year.
- The company is urgently hiring Drivers and Senior Loop Ops Managers for Vegas Loop.
- Application links were provided for Driver roles and Senior Loop Ops Manager roles based in Vegas.

## Context

Vegas Loop is The Boring Company’s underground transportation system in Las Vegas. The company has previously reported operational stations and entitled station totals through official channels.

## Limits of this report

This report contains only the details stated in the source post from @boringcompany. It does not specify the current operational station count, the exact number that will result from the doubling, construction timelines for individual stations, or hiring volumes.
