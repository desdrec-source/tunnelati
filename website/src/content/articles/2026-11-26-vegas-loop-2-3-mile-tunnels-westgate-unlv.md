On 26 November 2026 The Boring Company posted a video showing a drive through 2.3 miles of Vegas Loop twin tunnels, starting at the Westgate Resort and ending across from UNLV’s Thomas & Mack Center, with the route continuing toward the airport. The company stated that 14 additional Loop stations will open when these twin tunnels open to the public.

> 2.3 miles through Las Vegas, starting at the Westgate Resort and ending across from UNLV’s Thomas & Mack Center (and then to Airport). When these twin tunnels open to the public, 14 additional Loop stations open too.

## What was announced

- A 2.3-mile drive through Las Vegas Loop twin tunnels starting at the Westgate Resort and ending across from UNLV’s Thomas & Mack Center.
- The route continues toward the airport.
- When the twin tunnels open to the public, 14 additional Loop stations will open.

## Context

Vegas Loop is The Boring Company’s operational underground transportation system in Las Vegas. The company has previously stated that the station count will double by the end of the year and has listed current operational stations including the Westgate station.

## Limits of this report

This report contains only the details stated in the source post. It does not include an opening date for the twin tunnels, the identities of the 14 additional stations, current operational status of the tunnels, ridership metrics, or any other unstated information.
