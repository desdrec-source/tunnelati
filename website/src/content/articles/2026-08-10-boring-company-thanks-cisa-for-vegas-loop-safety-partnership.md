On August 10, 2026, The Boring Company posted on X thanking the Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (@CISAgov) for its role in safety and security efforts for the Vegas Loop.

> Thanks to @CISAgov for the great partnership in maximizing safety and security in Vegas Loop.

## What was announced
- The Boring Company publicly acknowledged a partnership with CISA.
- The stated goal of the collaboration is maximizing safety and security in the Vegas Loop.

## Context
The Vegas Loop is a tunnel-based transportation system developed by The Boring Company in Las Vegas.
