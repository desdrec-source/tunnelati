The Boring Company stated that Prufrock-5 is finishing a test-tunnel in Bastrop, Texas, and that the approximately 900,000 lb boring machine will re-launch in November.

> Prufrock-5 finishing a test-tunnel in Bastrop, Texas.   
>
> The ~900,000 lb boring machine will re-launch in November.

## What was announced

- Prufrock-5 is finishing a test-tunnel in Bastrop, Texas.
- The machine weighs approximately 900,000 lb.
- The machine will re-launch in November.

## Context

Prufrock is the name of The Boring Company’s tunnel boring machine series. The company has previously published updates on Prufrock machines operating at its facilities and on project sites.

## Limits of this report

This report contains only the details stated in the source post from @boringcompany. It does not specify the length of the test-tunnel, the purpose of the test, the destination of the November re-launch, or further technical specifications.
