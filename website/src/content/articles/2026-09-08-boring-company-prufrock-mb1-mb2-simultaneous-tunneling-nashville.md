The Boring Company posted drone footage on X showing Prufrock-MB1 and Prufrock-MB2 tunneling simultaneously in Nashville.

> Drone footage of Prufrock-MB1 and Prufrock-MB2 tunneling simultaneously in Nashville.  
>  
> The 10,000+ psi limestone is transported from each boring machine via a continuous conveyor belt, completing its journey to the rock pit via the 2 vertically stacked side belts seen below.

## What was announced

- Drone footage of Prufrock-MB1 and Prufrock-MB2 operating at the same time in Nashville.
- Limestone with compressive strength of 10,000+ psi is removed from each machine by continuous conveyor belt.
- The material travels to the rock pit via two vertically stacked side belts.

## Context

Prufrock machines are The Boring Company’s tunnel boring machines used on the Music City Loop project in Nashville. Prior official updates confirmed both MB1 and MB2 were actively tunneling.

This report contains only the details stated in the source post. It does not include tunneling rates, remaining distance, station progress, or other project metrics.
