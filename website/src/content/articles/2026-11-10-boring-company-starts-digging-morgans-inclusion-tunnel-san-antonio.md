On 10 November 2026 The Boring Company posted that it has officially started digging the Morgan’s Inclusion Tunnel in San Antonio.

> What an inspiring event and a welcoming community.
>
> TBC is proud to partner w Morgan’s and have officially started digging the Morgan’s Inclusion Tunnel.

## What was announced

- The Boring Company confirmed that digging has officially started on the Morgan’s Inclusion Tunnel.
- The post quoted a Morgan’s announcement of a ceremonial start of the machine for the nearly mile-long connection across the campus.
- A related post the same day stated that 29 days had elapsed from completion of tunneling in Bastrop to initiation of tunneling in San Antonio.
- Another related post stated that all Prufrocks are fully reusable and that Prufrock-1 has constructed 8 tunnels.

## Context

The Boring Company previously reported that Prufrock-5 had arrived at Morgan’s Inclusion Campus in San Antonio to construct a 0.8-mile tunnel. The company’s official project page lists Morgan’s Inclusion Tunnel as under construction in San Antonio.

## Limits of this report

This report covers only the statements made in the cited source posts from @boringcompany. It does not include construction timelines, costs, ridership projections, station designs, or details from other parties.
