The Boring Company announced on August 20 that it is hiring for its Music City Loop project in Nashville, with an in-person hiring event scheduled for Friday, August 28.

> Nashville - we are hiring! 
> Hiring Event: Friday, August 28
> Two TBMs are currently tunneling, with a 3rd arriving soon.

## What was announced
- The company is seeking candidates who can wrench, weld, wire, engineer, or manage construction sites.
- Applicants are directed to apply via a linked form for the Music City Loop Team.
- Two tunnel boring machines are already operating, with a third scheduled to arrive soon.

## Context
The Boring Company develops underground transportation tunnels. Music City Loop refers to its planned tunnel network in Nashville, similar to other projects such as the Vegas Loop.

## Limits of this report
This report contains only the details stated in the source post. It does not include information on the number of openings, compensation, specific roles, project timelines, or future plans.
