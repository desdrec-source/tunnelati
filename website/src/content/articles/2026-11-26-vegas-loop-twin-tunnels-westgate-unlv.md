On 26 November 2026 The Boring Company posted video of a 2.3-mile drive through twin tunnels in Las Vegas, starting at the Westgate Resort and ending across from UNLV’s Thomas & Mack Center, with continuation toward the Airport. The post stated that when these twin tunnels open to the public, 14 additional Loop stations will open as well.

> 2.3 miles through Las Vegas, starting at the Westgate Resort and ending across from UNLV’s Thomas & Mack Center (and then to Airport). When these twin tunnels open to the public, 14 additional Loop stations open too.

## What was announced

- 2.3 miles of twin tunnels have been driven, starting at the Westgate Resort and ending across from UNLV’s Thomas & Mack Center, with further connection toward the Airport.
- When these twin tunnels open to the public, 14 additional Loop stations will open at the same time.
- The post included video of the tunnel drive.

## Context

Vegas Loop is The Boring Company’s operating underground transportation system in Las Vegas. Official pages have previously listed stations including Westgate and noted approved tunnel mileage and station counts for the system. Earlier company updates have referenced progress toward airport connections and year-end station expansions.

## Limits of this report

This report contains only the details stated in the source post. It does not include opening dates, exact station names for the 14 additional stations, current operational status of these tunnels, passenger volumes, or any unstated construction metrics.
