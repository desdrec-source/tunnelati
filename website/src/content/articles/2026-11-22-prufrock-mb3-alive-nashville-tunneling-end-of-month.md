On 22 November 2026 The Boring Company posted that Prufrock-MB3 is alive in Nashville, with one week of commissioning and close-out remaining before it tunnels by the end of the month, and that Prufrock-MB4 arrives in December and will tunnel in January.

> Prufrock-MB3 is alive in Nashville!  
>
> One week of commissioning/close-out, and will be tunneling by end of month.  Two of the four grippers are visible on the right, each which push laterally with ~1 million pounds of force. 
>
> Prufrock-MB4 then arrives in December and will be tunneling in January.

## What was announced

- Prufrock-MB3 is alive in Nashville.
- One week of commissioning and close-out remains, after which it will be tunneling by the end of the month.
- Two of the four grippers are visible in the accompanying video, each pushing laterally with approximately 1 million pounds of force.
- Prufrock-MB4 arrives in December and will be tunneling in January.

## Context

Music City Loop is The Boring Company’s privately funded underground transportation project in Nashville. Earlier official posts stated that Prufrock-MB3 had arrived as the third tunnel boring machine for the project and that Prufrock-MB1 and Prufrock-MB2 were already tunneling.

## Limits of this report

This report contains only the details stated in the source post from @boringcompany. It does not include exact launch-site location, current tunneling distance, specific commissioning steps, or any performance data beyond the gripper force figure given.
