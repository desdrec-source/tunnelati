The Boring Company posted a video on X of Prufrock-MB2 tunneling on a Friday night in Nashville.

The official video is embedded above from the source post.

> Friday night tunneling in Nashville!
>
> Prufrock-MB2 is boring through the Music City limestone using 1,000,000+ lb of axial thrust, and 4,000,000+ lb of lateral gripping force.

## What was announced

- Video of Prufrock-MB2 operating at night in Nashville.
- The machine is boring through Music City limestone.
- Axial thrust exceeds 1,000,000 lb.
- Lateral gripping force exceeds 4,000,000 lb.

## Context

Prufrock-MB2 is one of the tunnel boring machines operating on the Music City Loop project in Nashville. Prior official posts confirmed simultaneous tunneling by MB1 and MB2.

## Limits of this report

This report contains only the details stated in the source post. It does not include tunneling rates, total distance completed, station progress, or other project metrics.
