The Boring Company stated that Prufrock-5 has arrived at Morgan’s Inclusion Campus in San Antonio. The 900,000 lb tunnel boring machine will construct a 0.8-mile tunnel to provide safe, weatherproof and convenient transportation for people of all abilities to move throughout the campus. Work is expected to begin following final permit approvals.

>

## What was announced

- Prufrock-5 has arrived at Morgan’s Inclusion Campus in San Antonio.
- The TBM weighs 900,000 lb.
- It will construct a 0.8-mile tunnel.
- The tunnel is intended to provide safe, weatherproof and convenient transportation for people of all abilities to move throughout the campus.
- Work is expected to begin following final permit approvals.

## Context

Prufrock is the name of The Boring Company’s tunnel boring machine series. The company previously reported that Prufrock-5 finished a test-tunnel in Bastrop, Texas, and would re-launch in November.
