On 28 October 2026, The Boring Company posted a video introducing the Operations Team in its Global Ops Center in Bastrop, TX.

> Meet the Operations Team in the Global Ops Center in Bastrop, TX!
>
> Today, the team is remotely controlling machines in Vegas (tunneling), Nashville (tunneling), and San Antonio (commissioning; not yet tunneling).

## What was announced

- The Operations Team in the Global Ops Center in Bastrop, TX.
- The team is remotely controlling machines in Vegas (tunneling), Nashville (tunneling), and San Antonio (commissioning; not yet tunneling).

## Context

The Boring Company maintains a Global Ops Center in Bastrop, Texas, from which it has previously stated that vehicles and machines can be remotely piloted.
