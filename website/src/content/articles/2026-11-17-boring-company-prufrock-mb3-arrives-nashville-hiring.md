On 17 November 2026 The Boring Company posted that Prufrock-MB3 has arrived in Nashville as the third tunnel boring machine building Music City Loop, that Prufrock-MB4 arrives in the next 8 weeks, and that it is hiring with an in-person event on Tuesday 24 November.

> Nashville, we’re hiring! 
>
> Prufrock-MB3 has arrived (the third TBM building Music City Loop), and Prufrock-MB4 arrives in the next 8 weeks. 
>
> Come dig with us - Engineering, Ops, Weld, Maintenance, Electrical, Facilities, Safety, and Security.
>
> Hiring Event: Tuesday, November 24
>
> Apply here:

## What was announced

- Prufrock-MB3 has arrived in Nashville and is identified as the third TBM building Music City Loop.
- Prufrock-MB4 arrives in the next 8 weeks.
- The company is hiring for roles in Engineering, Ops, Weld, Maintenance, Electrical, Facilities, Safety, and Security.
- A hiring event is scheduled for Tuesday, November 24, with a link provided to apply.

## Context

Music City Loop is The Boring Company’s privately funded underground transportation project in Nashville connecting downtown areas to Nashville International Airport. Earlier official updates from the company stated that Prufrock-MB1 and Prufrock-MB2 were tunneling and that additional machines were planned.

## Limits of this report

This report contains only the details stated in the source post from @boringcompany. It does not include information on the specific location of the machines, tunneling progress distances, launch dates, number of openings, compensation, or any unstated project timelines.
