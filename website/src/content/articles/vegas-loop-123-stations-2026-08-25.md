On 25 August 2026 The Boring Company posted that Clark County had approved an additional 19 Vegas Loop stations.

The company stated: "Thank you to @ClarkCountyNV for approving an additional 19 Vegas Loop stations - Vegas Loop is now entitled for 123 stations!"

It added: "Because Loop is point-to-point with no intermediate stops, in the limit, one could have a Loop station in every driveway!"

This is the primary official source for the change in entitled station count.
