On 19 November 2026 The Boring Company posted that it and the Metropolitan Nashville Airport Authority have signed a 40-year agreement for a Music City Loop station.

> The Boring Company and Metropolitan Nashville Airport Authority have signed a 40-year agreement for a Music City Loop station. Great user experience - less than 10 minutes from downtown to a station located 22 feet from the terminal entrance.
>
> Thanks so much to @Fly_Nashville for the great partnership.

## What was announced

- A 40-year agreement has been signed between The Boring Company and the Metropolitan Nashville Airport Authority for a Music City Loop station.
- Travel time is stated as less than 10 minutes from downtown to a station located 22 feet from the terminal entrance.
- The company thanked @Fly_Nashville for the partnership.

## Context

Music City Loop is The Boring Company’s privately funded underground transportation project in Nashville. Earlier official updates from the company have confirmed that Prufrock-MB1 and Prufrock-MB2 are tunneling, with Prufrock-MB3 having arrived and Prufrock-MB4 planned. The Metropolitan Nashville Airport Authority previously approved a Music City Loop connection and station at Nashville International Airport.
