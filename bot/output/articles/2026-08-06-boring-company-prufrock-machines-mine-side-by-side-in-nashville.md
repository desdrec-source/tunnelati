The Boring Company announced via X on August 6, 2026, that its Prufrock-MB1 and Prufrock-MB2 tunnel boring machines are mining simultaneously in Nashville.

> Prufrock-MB1 and Prufrock-MB2 mining at the same time in Nashville.
> Tunnels are side-by-side, currently separated by ~20 feet laterally.

## What was announced
- Prufrock-MB1 and Prufrock-MB2 are mining at the same time.
- The tunnels are positioned side-by-side and separated laterally by approximately 20 feet.

## Context
The Boring Company designs and operates tunnel boring machines named Prufrock for urban tunneling projects, including work in Nashville.
