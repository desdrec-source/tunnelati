On August 25, 2026, The Boring Company posted that Clark County, Nevada had approved 19 additional Vegas Loop stations.

> Thank you to @ClarkCountyNV for approving an additional 19 Vegas Loop stations - Vegas Loop is now entitled for 123 stations!

## What was announced
- Clark County approved 19 more Vegas Loop stations.
- The project is now entitled for a total of 123 stations.
- The post notes that Loop stations are point-to-point with no intermediate stops.

## Context
The Boring Company develops underground transportation tunnels. Vegas Loop is its project serving the Las Vegas area.

## Limits of this report
This report covers only the stated approval and total station count. It does not include timelines, costs, ridership figures, or future expansion plans.
